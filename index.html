ABOUT PROJECT 3 WHEEL

Modernizing Electric Mobility & Garage Eco-System

Project 3 Wheel is Bangladesh's pioneering operating platform purpose-built for electric rickshaws, mishuks, easy-bikes, and charging garage hubs.

100% Financial Transparency

Eliminate paper khata books, missing dues, and cash disputes. Every taka of daily rental deposit, garage rent deduction, and maintenance cost is logged with cryptographic certainty.

Built for EV 3-Wheelers

Tailored specifically for battery charging garages, 48V/60V lead-acid & lithium units, motor servicing labor, and daily garage rental accounting.

Smart Pilot Dignity & Security

Equip drivers with high-resolution digital QR Pilot IDs, verifiable in real-time by traffic authorities, garage staff, and fleet owners.

POWERFUL CAPABILITIES

Everything You Need to Run Your Fleet Hub

A complete all-in-one suite covering collection logs, expense tracking, shareholder dividends, driver management, and GPS intelligence.

Automated Daily Joma & Rent

Instant 1-tap logging for Full, Partial, or Unpaid deposits. Automatic daily garage rent and overhead cost deductions keep net profit calculations 100% exact.

Live GPS & Route Telematics

Simulated and live GPS tracking for every vehicle. Monitor speed, battery SoC voltage, route coverage, and get instant geofencing security alerts.

Shareholder & Equity Ledger

Multi-partner garage fund management. Record equity percentages, track fund injections with notes, and distribute net profits transparently.

Instant QR Pilot ID Cards

Generate high-resolution printable PDF ID cards with scannable QR codes for every driver. Quick scanner opens the collection form with zero typing.

Smart Expense & Garage Fund

Categorize parts, mechanic labor, power bills, and line fees. Automatic deduction from the Garage Fund when expenses are paid from common pool.

1-Click P&L & Excel Export

Generate formal PDF financial statements, monthly P&L audits, and export complete Excel spreadsheets for bank reconciliations and partner meetings.
